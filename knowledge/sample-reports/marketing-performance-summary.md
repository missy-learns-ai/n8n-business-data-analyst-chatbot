## Interpretation

Spend shows budget allocation. It does not by itself show efficiency.

ROAS, conversion rate, cost per click, and cost per conversion should be used when the question asks about performance efficiency.

## Recommended Follow-Up

Useful follow-ups include comparing spend and ROAS by channel, checking conversion rate by device, or reviewing revenue by audience segment.
